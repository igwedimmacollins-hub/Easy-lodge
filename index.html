Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common concerns.

Is water supply really constant? +
Yes. All our lodges have borehole systems with overhead tanks. Water runs daily.
How far are the lodges from UNIZIK campus? +
Most lodges are within a 5–10 minute walk to the main gate or faculty gates. No bike needed.
Can I pay in instalments? +
Absolutely. Our Flexi Pay plan lets you split payments. Contact us to discuss a schedule.
Is there a refund if I change my mind? +
We offer a 7-day grace period after move-in. Terms apply. Reach out for details.
